About 1 minute. Every year Indian families send lakhs of rupees abroad for tuition. Before they pay, they have to work out four things on their own: how much of the yearly LRS limit is left, how much TCS tax the bank will add, which bank or provider is really cheapest once the hidden exchange-rate markup is counted, and which papers the bank wants. One mistake costs money. TuitionPilot is an AI agent that does this work, explains every number, and pays only inside a simulated bank after the parent enters a PIN and the parent's own device digitally signs the exact plan. Everything you will see uses synthetic data, no real money, and it is not tax advice.
