About 1 minute 15. This is the part for the bankers and academics on the panel. The Liberalised Remittance Scheme lets each person send up to USD 250,000 a year abroad, counting all purposes together. Tax collected at source, TCS, is collected by the bank on top. There is no TCS on the first 10 lakh rupees a person sends in a financial year. Above that, for education paid from your own money, the rate is 2 percent from 1 April 2026; it was 5 percent before. If the education is paid with a loan from a notified institution, TCS is zero. Our rule pack stores every one of these with its value, the date it took effect, its source and its status. Be honest here: the threshold and the education rates come from secondary sources, and four rules are still unverified, shown in orange. Every output that uses an unverified rule says so. The engine never hard-codes a rate, and it refuses to calculate for a date before a rule took effect.
