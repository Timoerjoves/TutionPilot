How it works

Eight steps. Nothing moves without you.

01
Consent
You choose what the agent may do.
  1. STEP 01

    Consent

    Pick what the agent may read and save. It can never pay a new payee, raise a limit or skip your approval.

    T0 read · T4 never
  2. STEP 02

    Upload invoice

    Drop the university's invoice as a PDF or JSON file. Personal numbers are masked before any AI reads it.

    masked text only
  3. STEP 03

    Verify signature

    The university signed its bank details. Change one digit of the account and the signature breaks, so a fake "new bank details" email fails.

    Ed25519 · RFC 8032
  4. STEP 04

    Questions

    Earlier remittances this year, loan or own funds, PAN. The agent asks only what it cannot read, and never guesses.

    no guessing
  5. STEP 05

    Compare routes

    Each bank and provider is costed against the mid-market rate, so the markup hidden inside the exchange rate shows up in rupees.

    T1 compute
  6. STEP 06

    Documents & A2 form

    A filled A2-style draft, the LRS and TCS working with a source for every rule, and a checklist of what is still missing.

    T2 draft
  7. STEP 07

    Review & sign

    A parent's device signs the exact plan. If anything changes afterwards, even one rupee, the signature no longer matches and the bank refuses.

    T3 parent only
  8. STEP 08

    Receipt

    The sandbox bank pays exactly once and returns a receipt. Every step sits in a hash-chained audit log you can verify.

    audit chain
